What the photometry alone determined
  Spin: only P_A/2. The target is 2-fold symmetric about the spin axis, so
  consecutive 0.9248 s cycles are identical and the light curve carries no
  information distinguishing them. This is the correct result, not a failure --
  Rev B section 9.2 is why Model B exists. The slot sensor picks the branch.
  Precession: only P_B/2. The rock is symmetric about the plane-of-sky
  position, so rock angles +eps and -eps present near-identical aspect and the
  envelope repeats every 5.704 s. See pipeline/README.md on offsetting
  the sweep centre if the full P_B is wanted from photometry alone.

'dev' is (recovered - expected) / sigma. The slot-sensor row is an
